Artificial intelligence inference is the process of running a trained machine learning model to generate predictions or outputs from new input data. Unlike training, which requires massive computational resources to update model weights over large datasets, inference focuses on efficiently executing a fixed model to produce results in real time or near-real time. As AI models have grown from millions to hundreds of billions of parameters, the challenge of performing fast, cost-effective inference has become one of the central engineering problems in the field. Large language models such as GPT, LLaMA, Qwen, and DeepSeek are autoregressive transformer architectures.
